# Code of Conduct

VAPT Sanitizer welcomes constructive technical collaboration.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on technical content;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks;
- respect authorization boundaries in security-testing discussions;
- never post real client secrets or confidential assessment data;
- give maintainers and contributors reasonable space to disagree on design and risk tradeoffs.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that exposes sensitive data or violates these expectations, and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its community.

For conduct concerns that should not be public, use a private repository contact mechanism where available.
